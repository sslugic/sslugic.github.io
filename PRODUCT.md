## Platform

web

## Users

- **Hiring and leadership evaluators:** recruiters, founders and executives judging Slaven Slugic for engineering leadership roles. They skim for seniority, scope, shipped results and how to reach him.
- **Founders and investors:** startups looking for an angel investor, plus real estate contacts. They want to see judgment, what he builds, and a direct line to him.

## Product Purpose

Slaven Slugic's personal site (https://sslugic.github.io). It presents, in balanced measure: twenty years of engineering and engineering-leadership work, the products he builds on his own, his real estate and angel investing, his book, and how to contact him. Success is a visitor who understands who he is within a minute and reaches out.

## Positioning

A hands-on engineering leader who still ships his own products: agentic QA (Testera, Testera Probe), local agent memory (amem), live meeting context (Undertone), an AI document archive (Docklio), a daily good-news edition (The Whole Day) and a retro fitness app (Pulse), while leading engineering in stablecoin and payments infrastructure and investing in property and startups.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, product sites, a shared link or a scanned QR code, often on a phone.
- Contact paths: email slugics@gmail.com, phone (786) 796-7801, LinkedIn, GitHub, and a downloadable vCard (assets/slaven-slugic.vcf) also encoded in an on-page QR code.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single static `index.html` plus `assets/`, no build step, deployed by pushing `main` to GitHub Pages (repo sslugic/sslugic.github.io).
- External scripts: qrcode-generator from cdnjs; fonts from Google Fonts.
- Content is factual and owned by Slaven; new claims, numbers or testimonials need his approval.

## Brand Commitments

- Dark designs only; Slaven dislikes light themes.
- His portrait photo stays in the first screen.
- The digital contact card with QR code stays.
- He likes black with orange, and the green monospace "Terminal" look.

## Product Principles

1. Balanced: career, products, investing and writing each get their due; none buries the others.
2. Show the work: real screenshots and real links over adjectives.
3. Reachable in one step from anywhere on the page.
4. Personal: Miami, the outdoors and a builder's curiosity, not a corporate résumé.
